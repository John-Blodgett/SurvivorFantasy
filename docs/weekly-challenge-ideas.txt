WEEKLY CHALLENGE IDEAS
======================

Reference list for creating weekly challenges by hand.

Type key:  MC = multiple choice, DD = castaway dropdown, FR = free response
Points:    3 = most players should get it, 5 = roughly a coin flip, 7 = hard

Left out on purpose because they are easy to spoil: who is voted out, who wins
the season, anything about the first scene, who wins the immunity challenge,
which tribe loses, and who gets the first vote reveal.

Words in [brackets] are placeholders to fill in.


PAST CHALLENGES (from this league)
----------------------------------

Will anyone cry?
  MC: Yes / No | 3 pts
  Includes next time on survivor scenes.

How many times will "[phrase]" be said?
  MC: 0-1 / 2-3 / 4-5 / 6-7 / 8+ | 5 pts
  Has to be the full phrase but includes anytime they are said together for
  the full episode.

Who will be chosen to visit the opposing tribe?
  DD | 5 pts
  2 correct answers. One from each tribe.

Who will get the most confessionals?
  DD | 5 pts
  Ties will also count as wins.

Who says the quote that is the name of the next episode?
  DD | 5 pts
  The name of the episode is "[episode name]".

How many ads will there be to apply to Survivor tonight?
  MC: 0 / 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 / 5 / 6 / 7 / 8 | 5 pts

Who is going to be first to LOSE the individual immunity challenge?
  DD | 5 pts

Other than fish, whales, and birds, name one animal that will be shown in the
next episode.
  FR | 5 pts
  Name a general kind of animal, like "snake," "crab," or "dolphin." You don't
  need the specific species. Group names like "bug," "reptile," or "insect" are
  too broad and won't count. Fish (anything with gills), whales, and all birds
  are off limits. The animal has to be clearly visible on screen in the episode
  itself. Insects count if you can clearly see them. An animal that is only
  shown after it has been cooked doesn't count.

If the coin is being flipped, who's flipping it?
  DD | 3 pts
  (Specific to the million dollar coin twist.)

Is the million dollar coin being flipped?
  MC: Yes / No | 3 pts
  Only counts if a contestant chooses to flip it themselves. If it's just
  flipped for fun after, it doesn't count.
  (Specific to the million dollar coin twist.)

Will the individual immunity challenge last over or under 90 minutes?
  MC: Over 90 minutes / Under 90 minutes | 5 pts
  The over will only count if it's confirmed that the challenge went over that
  time limit. Otherwise the under will win. It only counts if the actual
  episode confirms this. Does not count if it's confirmed from bonus scenes or
  confirmed after the fact by a player.


EPISODE EVENTS (yes / no)
-------------------------

Will an idol or advantage be played at tribal council?
  MC: Yes / No | 5 pts

Will a castaway find a hidden idol on screen?
  MC: Yes / No | 5 pts

Will anyone quit or be medically evacuated?
  MC: Yes / No | 7 pts

Will there be a tied vote at tribal council?
  MC: Yes / No | 7 pts

Will anyone catch a fish or other food on screen?
  MC: Yes / No | 5 pts
  The catch itself must be shown. Seeing a rod, net, or bait is not enough.

Will there be a rain scene?
  MC: Yes / No | 5 pts
  Rain must be visibly falling on screen.

Will a castaway make fire on screen?
  MC: Yes / No | 5 pts
  A castaway visibly uses flint or another tool to start a fire.

Will Jeff announce a new twist?
  MC: Yes / No | 5 pts
  A new rule or advantage is announced by Jeff during the episode.


COUNTS
------

How many castaways will receive votes at tribal council?
  MC: 1 / 2 / 3 / 4+ | 5 pts

How many confessionals will the top castaway get?
  MC: 3 or fewer / 4-5 / 6-7 / 8+ | 7 pts
  Uses the confessional count tallied by the league admin.

How many times will Jeff say "the tribe has spoken"?
  MC: 0 / 1 / 2+ | 3 pts


WHO IS IT (castaway dropdown)
-----------------------------

Who gets the first confessional of the episode?
  DD | 7 pts

Who gets the last confessional of the episode?
  DD | 7 pts

Who will Jeff speak to first at tribal council?
  DD | 7 pts
  The first castaway Jeff addresses directly after tribal council begins.

Who finds a hidden idol, if one is found?
  DD | 7 pts
  If no idol is found on screen, no answer is correct.

Who is shown making fire first, if anyone is?
  DD | 7 pts
  If nobody makes fire on screen, no answer is correct.

Who is the last castaway on screen before the closing credits?
  DD | 7 pts

Who wears a hat or bandana at tribal council?
  DD | 5 pts
  Worn on the head during the tribal council scene.


TRIVIA
------

What kind of immunity challenge will it be?
  MC: Endurance / Puzzle / Obstacle course / Balance / Other | 5 pts
  Based on what Jeff says or what is clearly shown. "Other" covers anything
  else.

What will the reward be?
  MC: Food / Comfort items / Journey / Other | 5 pts

Name one food that will be shown on screen.
  FR | 5 pts
  Name a general food, like "rice," "coconut," or "fish." It must be clearly
  visible in the episode.
